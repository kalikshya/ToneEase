About

ToneEase v1.0

ToneEase
AI-powered tone detection for better communication

What it does

ToneEase analyzes your messages in real time and suggests kinder, more professional rewrites - helping you communicate better across platforms.

Supported platforms

WhatsApp Web, Facebook Messenger, Instagram Direct, and any text input via the extension popup.

How it works

As you type, ToneEase detects the emotional tone of your message. If it finds harsh or negative language, it suggests a rewritten version that conveys the same meaning more politely. You can accept or reject any suggestion.
