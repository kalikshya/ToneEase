About
ToneEase v1.0
ToneEase
Version 1.0 · Chrome Extension
AI-powered tone detection for better communication

What it does

ToneEase analyzes your messages in real time and suggests kinder rewrites - helping you communicate better across messaging platforms.

How it works

As you type, ToneEase detects the emotional tone of your message. If it finds harsh or negative language, it suggests a rewritten version that conveys the same meaning more politely. You can accept or reject any suggestion - and everything is saved to your history.

Supported platforms

WhatsApp Web, Facebook Messenger, Instagram Direct, and any text input via the extension popup.
